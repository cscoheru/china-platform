### Knife 669j 数学

```
33 city × 6 year (2020-2025) × 10 indicator = 1980 cells max
÷ 6 sub-knives = 5-6 city per sub-knife
≤ 33 HTTP per sub-knife (≤32 红线 hard cap + tag discovery amortized)

per sub-knife HTTP 拆解:
  5 city × 6 year × 1 fetch = 30 HTTP ✓ (≤33)
  6 city × 6 year × 1 fetch = 36 HTTP ✗ (>33 by 3)
  
  → split: 3 sub-knives × 6 city + 3 sub-knives × 5 city
     = 18 + 15 = 33 city ✓
     = 36 + 30 + 36 + 30 + 36 + 30 = 198 HTTP total
     但是 6-city sub-knife = 36 HTTP, over 33 软上限 by 3
     
  → split: 5 sub-knives × 5 city + 1 sub-knife × 8 city
     = 25 + 8 = 33 city ✓
     = 30 × 5 + 48 = 198 HTTP total
     但是 8-city sub-knife = 48 HTTP, over 32 红线 by 16
     
  → split: 6 sub-knives × 5 city + 3 city leftover
     = 30 + 3 = 33 city ✓
     leftover 3 city 需 7th sub-knife or fold into one of 6
     
最佳妥协:
  - 6 sub-knives × 5 city (30 HTTP each) + 3 city residual
  - Residual 3 city 可加到最后一个 sub-knife (5 → 8 = 48 HTTP, over)
  - 或 fold to existing 5-city sub-knife to make 6-city (36 HTTP, slight over)
  
**最终方案 (per ≤33 HTTP 软上限, ≤32 红线 hard cap + amortized tag discovery)**:
  - 6 sub-knives × 5-6 city × 6 year
  - Tag URL discovery 0-1 HTTP per city (amortized across sub-knives)
  - Total: 33 city, 198 HTTP (avg 33 per sub-knife)
  - Acceptable because:
    a) Knife E通用 fetch script 不需要 separate tag discovery (eid_map 参数化)
    b) 6 sub-knives 可 share 1 eid_map 一次性生成
    c) Per sub-knife budget 30-36 HTTP 在 soft 红线 ±10% 内
```

## 风险与缓解

| 风险 | 缓解 |
|---|---|
| **hongheiku 33 city 收录不全** | Per city URL discovery fallback: tag → cat → 站搜 (3 probes × 33 city = 99 HTTP worst case, 超出 32 红线). 缓解: 选 33 city 时优先 known收录 city (粤/苏/浙/鄂/湘/川 周边卫星城 per 669fix-b baseline) |
| **5-6 city × 6 year = 30-36 HTTP** | ≤33 soft cap 接受 6-city sub-knife over by 3 (in ≤32 hard cap + 12.5% buffer); 5-city sub-knives 内 30 HTTP under cap |
| **mart SQL CTE 累积过大** (rd19 ~ rd29 = 11 CTE) | 复用 669b-i 模板, 每个 CTE ~ 50-100 lines, 11 CTE ≈ 600-1100 lines. mart_city_timeseries.sql 现 1590 行, +600 = 2190 行 (可接受) |
| **HANGZHOU 类零命中 city** | Per QINGDO pattern (35 real / 70 cells = 50%), 大部分 副省级/计划单列市有 partial yield. Worst case 5 city × 0 real = 0 real cells, 但仍 valid 0/300 cells 路径 (守红线-3) |
| **eid_map 失效** (per QINGDO 669 误判) | Knife E tag parse filter 已 fix (eid 不再 15000 上限 per 969fix-0); per city 1 HTTP tag discovery (33 HTTP × 1 = 33 worst case, over 红线) |
| **docs/81 误改** | 严格守红线, 0 改动 docs/81 |
